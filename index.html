About Weeshlist

A gift box. That's the whole idea.

The icon says it plainly: something wished for, wrapped and waiting. Weeshlist exists to make every wish easy to save, easy to share, and easy to actually give — without the awkward "what do you want?" text.

The problem

Wishlists live in screenshots, notes apps, and memory

Most people track what they want across a dozen half-remembered places — a photo in Camera Roll, a link texted to a parent, an item added to a cart and forgotten. By the time a birthday comes around, nobody actually knows what to get, and duplicate gifts or generic ones fill the gap.

The approach

One link in, one clean wish out

Weeshlist is built around the moment you find something you want: paste the link, and the name, price, and photo appear on their own. Friends follow the list, see what's already been claimed, and mark items as gifted — so the surprise stays intact and nobody buys the same thing twice.

The team

Small, independent, and still shipping

Weeshlist is built by a small independent developer, including the Safari extension that lets you add a wish from any shop without ever opening the app — shipped because it's exactly the kind of friction a wishlist app shouldn't have.

What we optimize for

Four things we won't compromise on.

Speed

Save in seconds, not minutes

Paste a link and the details appear — no manual entry, no friction between wanting something and saving it.

Accuracy

Real product data, not guesses

Name, price, currency, and photo are read from the actual page, with AI cleaning up whatever the page leaves messy.

No duplicates

Everyone stays in sync

Mark an item as gifted and it updates for every friend viewing the list — no more two of the same present.

Privacy

Your wishlist is yours

Your data syncs to your account so it's there on every device — never sold, never used to build an ad profile.

Support

How can we help?

Answers to what we hear most. Can't find it here — email us directly.

How does pasting a link actually work?
Paste a product URL into Weeshlist and it reads the page's own product data — title, price, currency, and image — and fills in a wish card automatically. If a shop's page is unusually structured, a one-tap "Generate" option uses AI to clean up the name, description, and price from what it can find.
How do I turn on the Safari extension?
In Safari, go to Settings → Extensions, enable Weeshlist, and allow it on the websites you shop from. A Weeshlist button then appears on product pages — tap it to add the page to your wishlist without opening the app.
Can my friends see what I've already been given?
Yes. Marking a wish as completed updates it for everyone following your list, so friends shopping for the same occasion can see it's already covered — without spoiling who got it for you.
Is Weeshlist free?
Yes — you can create wishes, follow friends, and try an AI-assisted paste for free. Weeshlist Premium removes limits and ads; exact pricing and any trial terms are shown in the app before you're asked to pay.
How do I find and follow a friend?
Everyone has a unique ID (UID) under "My UID" in the Friends tab. Share yours or enter a friend's to follow their wishlist — no phone number or contact syncing required.
Where is my data stored?
Your wishes sync to your account so they're available across devices and visible to the friends you choose to share with. See the Privacy Policy for the full breakdown of what's stored and why.
